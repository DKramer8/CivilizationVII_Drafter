Civ VII Draft

Settings
Draft Logic i Determines which civilization/leader combinations can be drafted based on selected matching rules.

Random: No pairing restrictions.
Historic: Leaders that are historically tied to the civilization. Some civilizations have no assigned leaders.
Geographic: Leaders that are associated with roughly the same region as the civilization. Some civilizations have no assigned leaders. Leaders can be linked to many civilizations (0 to all).
Strategic: Leaders that fit the civilization strategically. Leaders can be linked to many civilizations (0 to all).
Game Versions / DLCs i Filters the civilization and leader pools by owned game versions and DLC packs.
Ages i Filters the civilization pool by age.
Attributes i Filters civilizations and leaders by attribute points.

Filter applies to:
Civilization or Leader: Either one must satisfy the condition.
Civilization and Leader: Both must satisfy the condition.
Combined Civilization + Leader: The combined attributes must satisfy the condition.
Only Civilizations: Only the civilization must satisfy the condition.
Only Leaders: Only the leader must satisfy the condition.

Condition:
OR: At least one selected attribute must be present.
AND: All selected attributes must be present. (Each civilization and leader has exactly two attributes. Select at most 2 attributes, or 4 in combined mode.)
NOT: None of the selected attributes may be present.
Bans i Defines which civilizations and leaders are excluded from the draft pool.
Civilizations i Defines which civilizations are excluded from the draft pool.
Leaders i Defines which leaders are excluded from the draft pool.
